WHO WE ARE

Team Vayuputra

Team Vayuputra is a student-led engineering team from PSG Institute of Technology and Applied Research (PSG iTech), passionate about developing innovative drone technologies and exploring the possibilities of aerial robotics in space exploration.

Driven by a shared interest in aerospace engineering, robotics and emerging technologies, the team focuses on designing, developing and integrating unmanned aerial systems for challenging exploration environments. Through our participation in the International Space Drone Challenge (ISDC), we work towards understanding how aerial robots can contribute to future planetary exploration missions, particularly in environments inspired by Mars.

Our Mission

Team Vayuputra brings together multiple engineering disciplines to develop an integrated aerial robotic system. Our work involves:

Drone Design and Development

Designing and assembling multirotor platforms with carefully integrated propulsion systems, flight controllers and electronic components.

Computer Vision

Applying image processing and object detection techniques to help drones identify and interpret objects and environmental features.

Autonomous Navigation

Exploring robotics frameworks such as ROS 2 and simulation environments to develop intelligent navigation and mission execution capabilities.

Our Vision

We envision a future where aerial robotic systems play an important role in planetary exploration by reaching locations that traditional ground-based robots may find difficult to access. From surveying unfamiliar terrain to identifying objects and assisting future exploration missions, we aim to contribute to the development of intelligent and reliable aerial systems.

At Team Vayuputra, we believe in learning through experimentation, solving real-world engineering challenges, and transforming innovative ideas into practical technologies.
