TEAM RECRUITMENT WORKSHOP 2026

A workshop for students interested in joining Team Vayuputra, covering the fundamentals of drone mechanics and development while sharing our experiences and learning from participating in ISDC.

Team Recruitment Workshop 2026 was conducted for students interested in joining Team Vayuputra and becoming part of our drone development journey.

The workshop introduced aspiring team members to the fundamentals of drone mechanics, the role of different systems within a drone, and the engineering principles involved in designing, building, and operating one. The session was intended to give students a clear understanding of the technical foundation required to contribute to the team.

Alongside the technical introduction, we shared our experiences from participating in the International Space Drone Challenge (ISDC) . We discussed the practical challenges encountered during competition preparation, the process of developing and testing our systems, and the importance of collaboration across different engineering disciplines.

The workshop also gave interested students an insight into what it means to work as part of a competitive engineering team, from learning and experimentation to problem-solving, testing, and working together toward a common goal.

Through the session, we aimed to help students understand the opportunities within Team Vayuputra and encourage those interested in drone technology, engineering, and innovation to take their first step toward joining the team.
