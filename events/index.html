INDUSTRIAL VISIT

DE DRONE WORLD SOLUTIONS

ISDC 2026 taught our drone, and our team, some hard lessons. To find answers, Team Vayuputra made an industrial visit to De Drone World Solutions Pvt Ltd in Coimbatore on 15 October.

We were hosted by Wing Commander A Satish Kumar (Retd), Founder & CEO of De Drone World Solutions. He walked us through how a drone company operates, discussed the challenges we faced at ISDC 2026, and shared ideas and practical tips for our next build.

Three lessons stood out from the visit:

Every component has a purpose. Each part of a drone is chosen for a reason, and understanding that reason is what turns assembly into engineering.

Design is about trade-offs. Building a reliable drone within a strict weight budget depends on making smart design choices.

Build for a real need. Drone technology is worth building when it solves a real-world problem.

We thank Wing Commander A Satish Kumar (Retd) and the De Drone World team for their time and for offering us technical support in the future. We are taking these lessons straight into our preparation for ISDC 2027.
YUKTHA 2026

LET'S BUILD A DRONE

Yuktha 2026 – Let’s Build a Drone was a hands-on workshop conducted by Team Vayuputra to introduce students to the fundamentals of drone technology and the process of building and operating a drone.

The workshop brought together students from our institution as well as participants from various colleges and institutions. Through the sessions, participants were introduced to the different aspects involved in developing a functional drone, from understanding the overall system and its components to assembly, integration, and flight.

Beyond the theoretical sessions, the workshop focused strongly on practical learning. Participants were given hands-on exposure to the drone-building process and were introduced to the principles behind stable flight and hovering. This provided them with an opportunity to connect the concepts discussed during the workshop with their practical application.

The session was designed to give students a broader understanding of drone technology while encouraging them to explore engineering, design, and autonomous flight.

Certificates of participation were presented to the participants upon completion of the workshop.
2026

TEAM RECRUITMENT WORKSHOP 2026

Team Recruitment Workshop 2026 was conducted for students interested in joining Team Vayuputra and becoming part of our drone development journey.

The workshop introduced aspiring team members to the fundamentals of drone mechanics, the role of different systems within a drone, and the engineering principles involved in designing, building, and operating one. The session was intended to give students a clear understanding of the technical foundation required to contribute to the team.

Alongside the technical introduction, we shared our experiences from participating in the International Space Drone Challenge (ISDC). We discussed the practical challenges encountered during competition preparation, the process of developing and testing our systems, and the importance of collaboration across different engineering disciplines.

The workshop also gave interested students an insight into what it means to work as part of a competitive engineering team, from learning and experimentation to problem-solving, testing, and working together toward a common goal.

Through the session, we aimed to help students understand the opportunities within Team Vayuputra and encourage those interested in drone technology, engineering, and innovation to take their first step toward joining the team.
