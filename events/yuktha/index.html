YUKTHA 2026

LET'S BUILD A DRONE

A hands-on drone-building workshop introducing students from our institution and various colleges to drone systems, assembly, integration, and flight.

Yuktha 2026 – Let’s Build a Drone was a hands-on workshop conducted by Team Vayuputra to introduce students to the fundamentals of drone technology and the process of building and operating a drone.

The workshop brought together students from our institution as well as participants from various colleges and institutions. Through the sessions, participants were introduced to the different aspects involved in developing a functional drone, from understanding the overall system and its components to assembly, integration, and flight.

Beyond the theoretical sessions, the workshop focused strongly on practical learning. Participants were given hands-on exposure to the drone-building process and were introduced to the principles behind stable flight and hovering. This provided them with an opportunity to connect the concepts discussed during the workshop with their practical application.

The session was designed to give students a broader understanding of drone technology while encouraging them to explore engineering, design, and autonomous flight.

Certificates of participation were presented to the participants upon completion of the workshop.
